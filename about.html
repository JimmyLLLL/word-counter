About Word Counter

Word Counter is a free online tool that counts words, characters, sentences, paragraphs, and lines in real-time. It also calculates reading and speaking time, and shows keyword density analysis.

Features

  • Live word, character, sentence, paragraph, and line counting
  • Reading time estimation (200 wpm)
  • Speaking time estimation (130 wpm)
  • Top 10 keyword density analysis
  • Copy and clear text functions
  • Works offline, no data sent to servers

Privacy

All text processing happens locally in your browser. Your text is never sent to any server or stored anywhere.
