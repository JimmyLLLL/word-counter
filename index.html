Free Online Word Counter

Our free word counter is a powerful tool for writers, students, bloggers, and professionals. Simply type or paste your text, and the tool instantly counts words, characters, sentences, paragraphs, and lines. It also calculates estimated reading and speaking time.

How Reading Time Is Calculated

Reading time is estimated at 200 words per minute, the average silent reading speed for adults. Speaking time is estimated at 130 words per minute, the average speaking rate for presentations. These are estimates — actual times vary by content complexity and reader.

How Sentences and Paragraphs Are Counted

Sentences are counted by detecting sentence-ending punctuation (periods, exclamation marks, question marks). Paragraphs are counted by detecting double line breaks. Lines are counted by detecting single line breaks.
